# Website audit — rodarlibre.co — 2026-11-12

Scope: the five public pages (`/`, `/aliados`, `/privacy`, `/allies-conditions`, `/delete-account`) plus
the internal `/og-test` page, audited on the built output (`pnpm build`, then inspecting `dist/`).
Status column: **Fixed** = fixed in the PR that adds this report; **Open** = left as a finding.

## How it was measured

- Lighthouse 13.6, mobile preset (simulated slow 4G, 4× CPU slowdown), headless Chrome, three runs per
  page, median reported.
- The site was served from `dist/` by a local static server. Two things differ from production and are
  called out where they matter: the local server compresses with gzip (production uses Brotli), and the
  Vercel Analytics script does not exist locally (it answers 404).
- Live checks with plain HTTP requests: store links, social links, redirects, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`
  and response headers on the production domain.
- Keyboard order and before/after screenshots were taken with headless Chrome at 390 × 844.
- Nothing here was checked on a physical phone. See "Not verified" at the end.

Notes on reading these numbers:

- **Best practices stays at 96 locally** because of one console error: the Vercel Analytics script is
  only served on Vercel. It is not a defect of the site; expect 100 on production.
- **Home FCP moved from 0.8 s to 0.9 s in the local run.** The home HTML grew by about 0.6 KB compressed
  (structured data and meta tags) and, with the local server's gzip, crossed the size that fits in the
  first network round trip (14.3 KB → 14.9 KB). With Brotli, which production uses, the same file is
  11.9 KB → 12.5 KB and stays under that size, so this should not show on the live site. Not verified on
  production because nothing was deployed.
- **One of the three "before" runs of the home scored 73** (840 ms of blocking time from the hero
  video starting under CPU throttling). The median hides it; it is listed below as an open finding.
- **SEO was already 100.** Lighthouse's SEO category only checks a handful of basics (title, description,
  crawlable links). The missing canonical, sitemap, robots file and per-page social tags below are not
  part of that score.

### Checked and fine

- Store links: App Store and Google Play both answer 200. Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and
  WhatsApp links are well formed; Instagram and Facebook block automated requests, so they were not
  confirmed by status code.
- Every external link that opens a new tab has `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- One `<h1>` per page and heading levels in order on all pages.
- All images have alt text; decorative copies in the allies marquee have empty alt.
- Fonts: two self-hosted WOFF2 files (79 KB together), preloaded, `font-display: swap`, no
  request to a third party.
- No layout shift (CLS 0–0.001).
- Allies marquee pauses on hover and focus and stops with "reduce motion"; carousel autoplay does too.
- HTTP → HTTPS and `www` → bare domain redirects work. HSTS is on.
- Text on production is compressed with Brotli; hashed assets are cached for a year.

## What riders search for that the site does not answer

Taken from what the existing pages say the app does. The site names each of these in one line and
answers none of them:

- How to check a vehicle's fines in SIMIT, and what the app shows once it finds them.
- When SOAT and the technical inspection expire, and how the reminders work.
- What Pico y Placa looks like in the app, in the rider's city and when travelling.
- What the Asistente de Tránsito IA is, who can use it and what it is not.
- What Go is. The word appears once, in the alt text of a screenshot.
- Whether the app is free, what data it asks for, where the vehicle data comes from, and whether it
  works for cars as well as motorcycles. The answers exist, spread across the privacy policy.
- How fuel yield (km per gallon), maintenance reminders and monthly cost are tracked.
- How to delete an account: answered, on `/delete-account`.

There is no FAQ on the site, so no `FAQPage` structured data was added.

## Proposed pages (outlines only, at most five)

None of these was written. Each outline must respect the copy rules: tú, plain words, no legal claim
or city rule that the site does not already make, no user numbers, no price for Go.

1. **`/preguntas-frecuentes` — Preguntas frecuentes** (with `FAQPage` structured data)
   - ¿Qué es Rodar Libre y para quién es?
   - ¿Sirve para carro y para moto?
   - ¿De dónde salen los datos de mi vehículo? (RUNT and SIMIT, as the privacy policy already says)
   - ¿Qué datos me pide y qué no recopila?
   - ¿Qué es el Asistente de Tránsito IA? (short answer, link to its page)
   - ¿Cómo elimino mi cuenta? (link to `/delete-account`)
   - ¿Cómo me vuelvo aliado? (link to `/aliados`)
2. **`/asistente-de-transito-ia` — Asistente de Tránsito IA**
   - What it is: an AI assistant for traffic questions, inside the app.
   - What it is not: it is not a lawyer and its answers are not legal advice.
   - Who can use it during the beta, and that the beta is free for a limited time.
   - What you should not type into it, and where your conversations go (link to section 6 of `/privacy`).
   - How to dictate a question.
   - Download buttons.
3. **`/multas-simit` — Revisa las multas de tu moto o carro**
   - What the app shows: fines and comparendos, vehicle by vehicle.
   - How to add a vehicle to your garaje.
   - Alerts when a new fine appears.
   - Where the information comes from (SIMIT), with a link to the official site.
   - Screenshot of the fines screen; download buttons.
4. **`/soat-y-tecnomecanica` — SOAT, tecnomecánica y licencia al día**
   - What the app brings from RUNT for your vehicle.
   - Reminders before each expiry date.
   - Licence by category and all-risk insurance: what you save and what the app counts down.
   - Screenshot of the dashboard; download buttons.
5. **`/pico-y-placa` — Pico y placa en la app**
   - What the app shows: the day your vehicle has a restriction, in your city or in another one when
     you travel (the claim the home already makes, no rule of any city stated).
   - Notifications the night before.
   - A line saying that the rule is set by each city and a pointer to the official source.
   - Screenshot of the notifications screen; download buttons.

## Not verified

- Nothing was tested on a physical phone: touch targets under a real thumb, the mobile menu, the
  carousel swipe, the hero video start, the "reduce motion" setting on iOS and Android, and the darker
  WhatsApp button under daylight.
- No screen reader (VoiceOver, TalkBack) session was run. The keyboard order was checked in headless
  Chrome only.
- Social cards were not checked in WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, X or LinkedIn; they read the live
  site, and nothing was deployed.
- Structured data was checked to be valid JSON-LD with the intended types; it was not run through a
  search engine's rich-results test, which also needs the live site.
- Lighthouse was run against a local server, not production. Field data (real visitors) was not
  consulted.
- The App Store link was checked by HTTP status, not by opening it on an iPhone.
- Safari and Firefox were not tested; all browser checks used Chrome.
